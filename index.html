Our Story

Founded in 2026, Nexus Book Club exists to create a welcoming community where readers learn from books and from one another.

More Than Reading

Every discussion at Nexus is an opportunity to explore ideas, appreciate different perspectives, and build genuine friendships.

We believe reading should not end when you turn the last page—it should continue through meaningful conversations that inspire action and personal growth.

To bring people together through books, thoughtful discussions, and shared experiences that encourage personal growth and lifelong learning.
